# Live visual and interaction notes

## Mobile product page

- Visual execution is polished, cohesive, and premium.
- First screen contains rating, order count, a large product image, TikTok proof, and the headline.
- Price and CTA require scrolling; the sticky CTA appears after the visitor has moved down the page.
- Offer cards and color choices are easy to scan once visible.
- The page displays Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and card marks, but does not advertise Klarna or Amazon Pay even though the checkout can expose them.

## Checkout

- Checkout leaves the branded domain and opens Stripe Payment Links.
- Merchant identity is displayed as “The cani project llc,” not Feels Like Om.
- Amazon Pay and Klarna are genuinely available on the live one-time checkout.
- Subscription checkout offers Klarna in its payment-method list and Amazon Pay as an express option.
- One-time checkout displays $134.99 even though the product page promises an automatically discounted $107.99 price.
- Subscription checkout displays $80 initially and then $39 every three months, while the storefront promises a six-month replacement cadence.
