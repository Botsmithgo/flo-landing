# Design and conversion review

The storefront presents the product at a premium level and has better message control than the current Amazon page. The visual system, photography, typography, offer cards, proof, guarantee, and limitation disclosures support a high-consideration purchase.

For TikTok mobile traffic, the first viewport delays price and purchase action. A visitor sees proof, product imagery, and the headline before the offer. The mobile landing experience should expose a price/offer CTA immediately or keep the sticky CTA visible from initial load.

The largest conversion risks are operational trust failures after the CTA: price mismatch, billing-cadence mismatch, corporate merchant name, broken policy pages, and a sandbox subscription portal. These will outweigh visual polish for buyers who reach checkout.
