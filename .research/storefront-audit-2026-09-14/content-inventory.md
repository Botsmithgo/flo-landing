# Storefront content inventory

## Strong conversion assets

- Clear hair/skin outcome in the headline.
- Strong product imagery and two finish options.
- 100,000+ orders shipped and 5M+ TikTok views.
- Amazon review excerpts and 4.8 rating claim.
- Sixty-day guarantee.
- Customer study with an explicit self-reported disclaimer.
- Direct explanation that the shower filter does not soften hard water.
- Comparison against a category leader and generic Amazon products.
- Installation, fit, filter-life, and product limitation content.

## Offer content

- Subscribe and save: $80 first order, shown against $134.99; storefront says the filter ships every six months.
- One-time: $107.99 first order, shown against $134.99; storefront says 20% is applied automatically.
- CTA sends the buyer directly to a Stripe Payment Link.

## Broken or inconsistent content

- One-time Stripe checkout displays $134.99, not the promised $107.99.
- Subscription checkout says $39 every three months, not every six months.
- Subscription checkout description contains “0-stage” and claims reduction of hard-water minerals.
- One-time checkout description calls the product a water softener and claims 98% chlorine removal, conflicting with the storefront's more careful language.
- The Stripe merchant identity is “The cani project llc,” creating a brand discontinuity.
- Shipping and return footer links both lead to 404 pages.
- Contact page uses caniprojectllc@gmail.com while the footer promotes hello@feelslikeom.shop.
- Manage-subscription link opens a Stripe sandbox portal.
